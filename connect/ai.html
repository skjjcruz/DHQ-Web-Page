Connect in two minutes

Claude Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise

  1. Open Claude → Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Dynasty HQ and paste this address:
https://hovnqztlbsgsywrbidbh.supabase.co/functions/v1/dhq-tools
  1. Leave the advanced settings alone and tap Add, then Connect.
  2. Sign in with your Dynasty HQ email and password, tick the leagues your AI may see, tap Allow.
  3. Start a new chat and ask: "Who should I start this week in my Psycho league?"

ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Team or Enterprise

  1. Open ChatGPT → Settings → Connectors (on some plans this is under Apps or needs Developer mode turned on in Settings → Connectors → Advanced).
  2. Choose Create (or Add custom connector), name it Dynasty HQ, and paste the same address:
https://hovnqztlbsgsywrbidbh.supabase.co/functions/v1/dhq-tools
  1. Authentication: OAuth. Leave the client fields blank; ChatGPT registers itself.
  2. Sign in with your Dynasty HQ email and password, pick your leagues, Allow.
  3. In a chat, turn on the Dynasty HQ connector and ask away.

Good to know

Read-only. Your AI can look, compare and recommend. It cannot add, drop, trade or set a lineup. You do that in Sleeper.

You choose the leagues. Untick any league on the sign-in screen and your AI never sees it. Disconnect any time from your AI's connector settings.

Signed up with Google? Set a password in Dynasty HQ → Settings first, then sign in here with your email and that password.

Numbers refresh every two hours; lineups, scores and injuries are checked live when you ask. New leagues get their first DHQ numbers within two hours of connecting.
